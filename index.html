How we work

The Sage Method*

A four-movement engagement rhythm — designed to be run on its own or embedded inside your existing programme. Every phase ends with something you can use, not just something to read.

“A restructure is a date on a calendar. Organisational change is what happens in the eighteen months after it.”Angela Peter · Founder & CEO

* Method name illustrative — refine with Angela before launch.

i

Diagnose

Stakeholder interviews, culture and readiness data, systems landscape. We map what is really true — not the official story.

ii

Design

Operating model, structure, change strategy and comms architecture — built with your leadership, stress-tested against reality.

iii

Deliver

Implementation support, training, sponsorship activation, resistance management. We run alongside your teams, not above them.

iv

Sustain

Adoption analytics, capability transfer, and a measure of what stuck. We hand over the instruments so the change keeps working without us.
